I

About

I study the foundations of mathematics, computer science, and economics — why methods work, and under what conditions.

My interests lie in the foundations of mathematics, computer science, artificial intelligence, and economics, with an emphasis on principles, rigorous reasoning, and understanding why methods work.

My current work connects mathematical foundations of learning and reasoning with uncertainty-aware multimodal methods.

Research interests

  • ∑

    Mathematics

    Mathematical principles, rigorous reasoning, and theoretical analysis.

    Computer science

    Fundamental principles of computation and algorithms, with an emphasis on understanding why and under what conditions methods work.

    AI principles

    Why learning systems work, what they can and cannot compute, and where their assumptions stop holding.

  • ⇌

    Economics

    Economic reasoning and models — incentives, choice, and the assumptions that make a model useful.
