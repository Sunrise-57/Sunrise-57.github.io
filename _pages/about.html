About

I study the foundations of mathematics and computer science — why methods work, and under what conditions.

My research interests lie in foundational research in mathematics and computer science, with an emphasis on mathematical principles, rigorous reasoning, and theoretical understanding.

My current work connects mathematical foundations of learning and reasoning with uncertainty-aware multimodal methods.
