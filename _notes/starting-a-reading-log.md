These pages are a running log of what I read — papers, talks, and the occasional book — and what I make of them. They are notes rather than reviews: written for myself first, published in case they are useful to someone else.

## What goes here

The subjects are deliberately broad: machine learning, deep learning, reinforcement learning, but also sociology and economics. The common thread is not a method but a question: **why does something work, and under which conditions does it stop working?** That question travels well between fields.

## What a note looks like

A note is usually short. It records what the paper claims, what I find convincing, and where I am still unsure. Some entries will be close readings with derivations; others will be a paragraph and an open question.

The aim is not a summary that could have been written without reading. It is a trace of my understanding at a point in time — which is allowed to be wrong later.

## What this section is not

It is not a review venue, not a literature survey, and not a claim to expertise. If something here is wrong, the error is mine and worth pointing out.

If you spot a mistake or disagree with something, [email me](mailto:wushiqi57520@gmail.com) — that is the fastest way to make the next note better.
